# Human Interface Quality Analysis

## Trigger

Use when a user-facing interface needs accessibility/usability coverage, including keyboard/focus, assistive semantics, input alternatives, reflow/contrast, error recovery, cognitive usability or externally imposed accessibility conformance.

## Inputs

- accepted users/journeys/product requirements;
- Interface Design contracts;
- Quality Design constraints;
- applicable Obligation Analysis;
- relevant accessibility/usability standards as coverage lenses;
- Verification/Test obligations.

## Read boundary

Accepted project design is authority. Standards and user-research findings improve recall or supply evidence; they do not silently create product requirements unless applicable through Product or Obligation ownership.

## Procedure

1. Identify user, journey/task and interface surface.
2. Identify accepted interaction semantics and required modes.
3. Apply accessibility/usability lenses to find material concerns.
4. Route user/scope requirements to Product.
5. Route interaction/presentation semantics to Interface Design.
6. Route measurable architecture-significant constraints to Quality Design.
7. Route external conformance duties to Obligation Analysis.
8. Route proof to Verification/Test.
9. Record COVERED, NOT_APPLICABLE, DEFERRED_NONBLOCKING or QUESTION.

## Stop conditions

Stop and route a QUESTION when target users, required interaction modes, conformance level, user-visible semantics or measurable acceptance criteria are missing and the analysis would otherwise invent them.

## Output contract

Produce interface-quality coverage by user/journey/surface, concern, owning Authority, accepted criterion, evidence obligation and coverage state.

## Acceptance checks

- criteria trace to accepted users/journeys or applicable obligations;
- accessibility is not reduced to automated linting;
- usability is not represented by a generic score without accepted meaning;
- Interface, Product, Quality, Obligation and Verification ownership remains distinct;
- standards remain lenses unless made applicable.

## Registration

Register as reusable cross-Authority analysis, not ACCESSIBILITY-DESIGN or USABILITY-DESIGN Authority.

## Human projection

Prefer concise coverage organized by journey/surface and routed gap, with explicit applicable/non-applicable criteria.
