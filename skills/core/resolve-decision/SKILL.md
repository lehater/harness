# Resolve Decision

1. Define one decision question and its owning truth layer.
2. Inspect relevant target-project canonical evidence before asking for a new decision.
3. Separate facts, constraints, proposals, hypotheses, unknowns and conflicts.
4. Identify credible alternatives, including the simplest viable option.
5. Compare consequences against accepted requirements/domain/architecture, risk and reversibility.
6. Recommend only when evidence supports a preference.
7. Record the accepted result in the target project's canonical owner or an ADR when durable rationale matters.
8. Propagate consequences and explicit revisit triggers.

Never convert a blocking unknown into a default.
